Welcome to AgroVision

AgroVision helps farmers and contractors by providing:

  • Revenue calculator (MSP + yield algorithm)
  • Crop planner with schedules
  • Dual portals for farmers and contractors

Click "Open Dashboard" to manage and use the tools.

API Endpoints

  • POST /api/crop-planner
  • POST /api/portals/farmers
  • GET /api/portals/farmers
  • POST /api/portals/contractors
  • GET /api/portals/contractors
